AI study guide generator built on Flutter + Supabase Edge Functions. Integrates OpenAI GPT-3.5 and Claude Haiku with a fallback chain to mock data. Supports English, Hindi, and Malayalam. Rate-limited anonymous access (3/hr) and OAuth access (30/hr) via Google and Apple Sign-In. 4-layer input validation pipeline with Row Level Security on PostgreSQL.
