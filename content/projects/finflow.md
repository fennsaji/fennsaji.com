A simple personal finance website for recording income and expenses, tracking accounts, and understanding where money goes. Supports CSV transaction import, monthly spending targets, credit-card outstanding and EMI tracking, and per-card statement views. Auth via email OTP or Google Sign-In on Supabase, built with Next.js.
